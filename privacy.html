Privacy, simply.

Effective October 29, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

Inside the game

Mist 2048 is an offline puzzle. The app does not collect or transmit personal information, gameplay records, device identifiers or analytics to the developer. It contains no account system, advertising, tracking SDKs, in-app purchases or third-party analytics.

Your board, scores and one-move undo history are stored in the app’s local sandbox. They are not sent to the developer or synced by the app. Deleting the app removes its local data. Apple’s device backup services may retain app data according to your system settings and Apple’s policies.

Email support

If you choose to email zengmmjs@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address and anything you include. This information is used to respond to your request, not for advertising or tracking. Please avoid sending sensitive information. You may request deletion of your support correspondence at the same address, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

This website and external services

This website uses no analytics scripts, advertising or cookies set by us. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure its hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Opening an email or website link uses your chosen external app and its privacy practices.

Children and changes

The game does not knowingly collect children’s personal information. This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change; the effective date will be revised. Contact the support address above with privacy questions.
