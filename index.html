How to play

Swipe inside the board to move the tiles. Matching numbers merge, adding to your score. Reach 2048 and keep going, or start fresh whenever you like.

Undo takes back one move; your best score stays. The board saves automatically on this device. No account, ads or purchases.

玩法

在棋盘内滑动，相同数字相遇时合并并加分。达到 2048 后可继续挑战。支持撤销一步，最高纪录会保留；进度自动保存在本机。无需账号，无广告、无内购。
